# Helm.ai

> Helm.ai is an AI software company for autonomous driving and robotics, founded in 2016
> and headquartered in Redwood City, California. It builds camera-first perception, path
> prediction and end-to-end autonomy stacks, plus generative AI tools for AV development
> and validation, trained with its proprietary Deep Teaching unsupervised learning method.
> The software is licensed to automotive OEMs and Tier 1 suppliers as embedded components
> of production vehicle programs.

## Products

- [Helm.ai Vision](https://helm.ai/products/perception): production-grade urban and highway
  perception for Level 2+ to Level 4. Multi-camera surround view, bird's-eye-view perception,
  semantic segmentation, 3D bounding boxes, distance estimation, mapless (no HD maps).
  Includes ISO 26262 ASIL-B(D) certified components; assessed at ASPICE Level 2.
- [Helm.ai Driver](https://helm.ai/products/helm-ai-driver): vision-only, real-time
  transformer-based path-prediction and end-to-end autonomy stack for Level 2+ through
  Level 4, powered by Factored Embodied AI. Camera-only — no HD maps or Lidar required.
- [GenSim-3](https://helm.ai/products/gensim): generative AV resimulation at native Full HD
  (2-megapixel), for scenario re-styling and closed-loop validation.
- [VidGen-3](https://helm.ai/products/vidgen): generative synthetic driving video for
  training and validation.
- [WorldGen-1](https://helm.ai/products/worldgen-1): multi-sensor generative foundation model
  of the driving world.

## Technology

- [Technology overview](https://helm.ai/technology): Deep Teaching, the unsupervised learning
  method behind the foundation models; also states the safety posture — compliance with
  ISO 26262 functional safety, adherence to ISO/PAS 21448 (SOTIF), and ASPICE integration.

Hardware-agnostic: runs on NVIDIA, Qualcomm, Texas Instruments and Ambarella platforms.
Named partners and customers include Honda Motor and Volkswagen.
